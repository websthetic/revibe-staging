Every January we hear the same thing at the front desk: "I had $500 of massage left and forgot about it."

If you want to use your health benefits before the end of the year, now is the time to check what's left. That money doesn't roll over. It's part of what you or your employer paid for this year, and once the plan year closes it's gone. If you've been putting off a nagging shoulder, a stiff back or the massage you keep meaning to book, December is the month to do it.

## How to check what you have left

Log in to your insurer's app or member site. Sun Life, Canada Life, Manulife, Desjardins and most others show a balance by benefit type. You're looking for three things.

**Your yearly maximum for each practitioner.** Plans often give a separate amount for physiotherapy, massage therapy, chiropractic and osteopathy, something like $500 each. Some combine them into one pot for all "paramedical" services.

**What the plan pays per visit.** A plan might cover 80% of each session up to a set dollar amount. That decides how much you pay at the desk.

**Your plan year.** Most run January to December. Some run on a different cycle, such as July to June. If yours does, this deadline doesn't apply to you yet.

If the app is confusing, call the number on the back of your card and ask: "How much do I have left for physiotherapy, massage therapy, chiropractic and osteopathy this year, and when does it reset?"

## How direct billing works

[We bill most insurers directly](/direct-billing/), so you don't pay the full amount and wait for a cheque. That goes for physiotherapy direct billing in Mississauga and massage therapy benefits in Markham alike.

1. Bring your insurance card and photo ID to your first session.
2. Check your benefits booklet or ask your insurer what your plan covers.
3. We submit the claim on the day. You pay the balance, if there is one.

You don't need a doctor's referral to book physiotherapy, massage therapy, chiropractic care or osteopathy at Revibe. Some insurers still want a referral on file before they'll pay for massage, so check that one if it applies to you.

## The mistake people make in December

They wait until the week before Christmas and then find every evening slot is gone.

If you have enough left for four sessions, spread them over the next five or six weeks. One session won't fix a problem you've had for months. Four, with the right plan between them, often makes a real difference, and your plan resets with you already moving better.
