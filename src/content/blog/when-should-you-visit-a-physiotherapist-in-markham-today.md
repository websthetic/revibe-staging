## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **How do I know if I should visit a Physiotherapist in Markham?**

If you have persistent pain, stiffness, reduced mobility, recurring injuries, or difficulty completing everyday activities, it may be time to schedule an assessment with a Physiotherapist in Markham.

### **Do I need a doctor's referral before booking physiotherapy?**

Many physiotherapy clinics accept patients without a referral, although some extended health insurance providers may require one for reimbursement. Check your insurance policy before your appointment.

### **How long does physiotherapy treatment usually last?**

The length of treatment depends on your condition, the severity of your symptoms, and your recovery goals. Your physiotherapist will monitor your progress and adjust your care plan as needed.

### **Can physiotherapy help prevent future injuries?**

Yes. Physiotherapy improves strength, flexibility, balance, and movement patterns, helping reduce the risk of future injuries while supporting long-term physical function.

### **What should I wear to my physiotherapy appointment?**

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move easily and provides access to the area being assessed or treated. Athletic or loose-fitting clothing is generally recommended.
