## **Common reasons insurance claims may be declined**

Even with direct billing massage Brampton services, insurance claims can sometimes be declined due to specific policy or administrative issues. ## **Cost breakdown and what you may still need to pay**

While direct billing massage Brampton services reduce the need for upfront payment, they do not always guarantee full coverage of treatment costs. Depending on your insurance plan, you may receive full coverage where the insurer pays the entire session fee directly to the clinic. However, many clients experience partial coverage where only a percentage or fixed amount per session is covered. 

In such cases, the remaining balance becomes a co-payment that must be paid by the client. Some insurance plans also have annual limits, meaning coverage may stop once the benefit amount is used up. Certain services or extended treatment durations may also fall outside coverage. Clinics usually inform clients beforehand so they understand any expected out-of-pocket expenses.
