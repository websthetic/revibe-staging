## **What Is Deep Tissue Massage?**

Deep tissue massage is a therapeutic technique that targets the deeper layers of muscles, fascia, and connective tissues to relieve chronic tension and improve mobility. Unlike relaxation massage, it uses slow, deliberate strokes and firm pressure to release tight muscles and reduce built-up stress within the body. Every session is customized based on your comfort level, physical condition, and treatment goals.

### **How often should I get deep tissue massage?**

Treatment frequency varies based on your needs, though many people benefit from regular sessions to maintain muscle health and flexibility.

### **Can deep tissue massage help with sports recovery?**

Yes. It helps reduce muscle soreness, improve flexibility, support recovery, and prepare muscles for future physical activity and performance.

### **Who should avoid deep tissue massage?**

Individuals with certain medical conditions, infections, fractures, or blood clotting disorders should consult a healthcare provider before treatment.
