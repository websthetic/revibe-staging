## WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW FOR YOUR FIRST SAUNA SESSION

ARE YOU NEW TO INFRARED SAUNAS? WE’VE GOT YOU COVERED WITH A FEW TIPS TO HELP YOU PREPARE FOR YOUR FIRST VISIT AND MAKE THE MOST OUT OF YOUR EXPERIENCE. HERE ARE SOME THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND:

1. **HYDRATE:**

    MAKE SURE YOU’RE HYDRATED BEFORE GOING INTO THE SAUNA AND BRING WATER WITH YOU IF YOU’RE SENSITIVE TO HEAT. YOU’LL ALSO NEED TO MAKE SURE YOU DRINK MORE WATER THAN USUAL THROUGHOUT THE DAY AFTER YOUR SESSION. WHEN IN DOUBT, DRINK MORE WATER!
2. **PAY ATTENTION:**

    DURING YOUR SESSION, PAY ATTENTION AND LISTEN TO YOUR BODY. DRINK WATER AS NEEDED, TAKE BREAKS, OR OPEN THE DOOR WHILE YOU’RE INSIDE FOR SOME FRESH AIR. YOU MAY ALSO NEED TO ADJUST THE TEMPERATURE SO THAT YOU FEEL MORE COMFORTABLE AS YOU ACCLIMATIZE. AVERAGE TEMPERATURES FOR INFRARED SAUNAS RANGE FROM 100°F TO 150°F. WE RECOMMEND THAT BEGINNERS START WITH 100°F. 
3. **POST-SESSION:**

    YOUR CORE TEMPERATURE WILL RAISE QUITE A BIT DURING YOUR SESSION SO TAKE EXTRA CARE GETTING UP AFTERWARDS TO PREVENT LIGHTHEADEDNESS, TAKE SOME TIME TO EASE BACK INTO THE REST OF YOUR DAY, REHYDRATE, AND CHANGE INTO COMFORTABLE CLOTHING AFTERWARDS.
