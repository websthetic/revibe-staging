## **Why Many People Prefer Infrared Saunas Over Traditional Saunas**

Traditional saunas heat the surrounding air to very high temperatures in order to warm the body. While many people enjoy this experience, others may find the intense heat uncomfortable or difficult to tolerate for long periods. Infrared saunas provide a different approach by heating the body directly rather than raising the air temperature.

Because of this difference, infrared sauna Mississauga sessions often feel more comfortable and manageable. The air remains at a lower temperature while infrared heat gently penetrates the body. This allows individuals to stay inside the sauna longer while still enjoying a deep warming sensation. Many people appreciate this balance because it offers the relaxing benefits of sauna therapy without overwhelming heat.
