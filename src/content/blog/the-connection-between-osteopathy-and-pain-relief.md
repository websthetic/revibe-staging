## **What is Osteopathy?**

[Osteopathy](https://revibeto.com/services/osteopathy/) is a form of manual therapy that uses gentle, hands-on techniques to diagnose and treat conditions affecting the body. Osteopaths aim to restore balance and harmony within the body, addressing the root causes of pain rather than just the symptoms. By improving blood circulation, enhancing lymphatic drainage, and promoting overall wellness, osteopathy provides a comprehensive approach to pain relief.

### **How Does Osteopathy Relieve Pain?**

Osteopathy addresses pain through various techniques, including structural, cranial, and visceral osteopathy. Each method targets different aspects of the body:

* **Structural Osteopathy:** This technique focuses on the musculoskeletal system, correcting imbalances in posture and alignment. By using gentle manipulations and soft tissue techniques, osteopaths can alleviate pain associated with injuries, arthritis, and chronic conditions.
* **Cranial Osteopathy:** A more subtle approach, cranial osteopathy involves gentle manipulations of the skull. This technique is particularly effective for relieving stress and tension, which can contribute to headaches and migraines.
* **Visceral Osteopathy:** This specialized technique addresses the internal organs and their connective tissues. By improving the mobility of these structures, visceral osteopathy can help alleviate digestive issues and other internal discomforts.

### **Benefits of Osteopathic Massage**

Osteopathic massage is an integral part of osteopathic treatment. This type of massage focuses on the body's tissues and aims to relieve pain through relaxation and improved circulation. Here are some key benefits of osteopathic massage:
