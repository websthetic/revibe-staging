Cold plunge therapy is a form of cold water immersion where the body is placed into chilled water for a short period. ## **How Cold Water Immersion Supports Muscle Recovery**

When the body is exposed to cold water, blood vessels temporarily narrow in a process called vasoconstriction. After leaving the cold environment, circulation gradually returns to normal. This natural response is one reason cold plunge therapy is commonly used after exercise.

This cycle may support recovery by:

* helping muscles transition from intense activity to rest
* reducing the feeling of heaviness after exercise
* supporting overall physical recovery after exertion
