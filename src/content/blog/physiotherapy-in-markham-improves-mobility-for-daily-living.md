## **Understanding Common Causes of Limited Mobility**

Limited mobility can develop gradually over time or occur suddenly after an injury, surgery, or illness. Although many people associate reduced movement with aging, it can affect individuals of all ages and activity levels. Common causes include muscle weakness, joint stiffness, arthritis, poor posture, repetitive movements, sports injuries, and prolonged inactivity. Workplace demands and post-surgical recovery can also contribute to restricted movement and discomfort. 

If left untreated, these issues may make everyday tasks such as walking, climbing stairs, or lifting objects increasingly difficult. Seeking Physiotherapy in Markham early can help identify the underlying cause and create a personalised treatment plan that restores mobility, improves flexibility, and supports better physical function before symptoms become more limiting.
