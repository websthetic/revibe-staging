## **Understanding What Causes Chronic Back Pain**

Chronic back pain is typically defined as pain that lasts for three months or longer, often affecting daily activities, work performance, and overall quality of life. Unlike temporary soreness that improves with rest, chronic pain may persist due to underlying muscle or soft tissue issues that require professional attention. Several factors can contribute to ongoing back discomfort, including poor posture, repetitive movements, heavy lifting, previous injuries, weak core muscles, age-related changes, and prolonged periods of inactivity. 

Stress can also increase muscle tension, making existing pain feel worse over time. As muscles become tight and less flexible, everyday movements such as bending, walking, lifting, or even sleeping may become uncomfortable. Recognizing these contributing factors early allows individuals to seek appropriate care and take steps toward improving mobility, reducing discomfort, and supporting long-term back health.

## **Massage Therapy Techniques Commonly Used for Back Pain**

Massage therapy is not a one-size-fits-all treatment, as every person experiences chronic back pain differently. An RMT in Mississauga will choose techniques based on your symptoms, health history, and treatment goals to provide personalized care. Common approaches include Swedish massage to promote relaxation and improve circulation, deep tissue massage to release long-standing muscle tension, trigger point therapy to address tight muscle knots that may cause referred pain, and myofascial release to improve flexibility by reducing restrictions in connective tissues. In many cases, these techniques are combined during a session to target multiple areas of concern. By tailoring treatment to your needs, massage therapy can help improve mobility, reduce stiffness, and support better back function while complementing other healthy lifestyle habits.
