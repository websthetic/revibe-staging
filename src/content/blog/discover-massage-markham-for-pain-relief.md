## **Popular Types of Massage Therapy Available**

Massage therapy includes several techniques designed to address different health goals and personal preferences. Swedish massage is commonly chosen for relaxation because it uses gentle strokes that improve circulation while easing muscle tension. Deep tissue massage targets deeper muscle layers to address persistent tightness and areas of chronic discomfort. 

Sports massage supports athletes and active individuals before or after physical activity to improve recovery and flexibility. Prenatal massage provides safe, supportive care for expectant mothers experiencing pregnancy-related discomfort. Trigger point therapy focuses on releasing tight muscle knots that may cause pain in surrounding areas. Your massage therapist can recommend the most appropriate technique based on your symptoms and treatment goals.
