## **Understanding How Direct Billing Works for Massage Therapy**

Direct billing is a convenient payment arrangement that allows massage clinics to handle insurance claims directly with a client’s benefits provider. Instead of paying the full session fee upfront and waiting for reimbursement, the clinic submits the claim electronically after the appointment is completed. With Direct Billing Massage Brampton services, this process is done through secure online insurance systems connected to participating providers. Once the claim is reviewed and approved, the insurance company sends payment directly to the clinic for the covered portion. If the plan only covers part of the treatment, the client pays the remaining balance. This system simplifies the entire process and removes the need for manual claim submission or waiting periods.
